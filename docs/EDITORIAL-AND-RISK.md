# Editorial rules and risk safeguards

## Positioning

Frontier is information and education. It explains an emerging technology, then follows the companies and the money.
It does not recommend any security, product or course of action, and it takes no account of any reader's situation.

## Safeguards built in

- A visible information-only notice at the top of every site, a "read this first" box above the projections, and a
  disclosures section at the foot.
- Projections are labelled scenarios. Probabilities and drifts are stated. Readers can set their own odds with the
  sliders. Volatility is each stock's own history, scaled and stated.
- A conflicts statement: Solas33 holds no position in any company named, takes no payment from any of them, and the
  only benefit to Solas33 is traffic to its own sites as an information provider. No advertising. Confirmed by the
  founder on 15 October 2026. If any of that changes, the statement is updated with the date.
- Every projection change is logged with its cause. Predictions are recorded in advance in a hash-chained ledger and
  scored later, and the record is shown on the page, including misses.
- Claims are graded on an evidence ladder: reproduced, peer-reviewed, contested, preprint, press release. Targets are
  labelled targets.
- News is headlines and links only, with the publisher named. No article text is copied.
- Photographs are public domain or CC0 only, credited under each picture, with a credits file kept in the repository.
- Corrections go to hello@solas33.com and are logged. Editorial responsibility: Solas33.

## Voice

The house style is in VOICE.md and is enforced by `tools/voice_lint.py`. The series does not describe how copy was
drafted. Someone at Solas33 reads every site before it is published; that review is what makes the page the publisher's
own.

## Legal

The founder has cleared the series legally (15 October 2026). Topic-specific points to re-check per site: market-abuse
rules on investment recommendations (all sites with listed companies), markets-in-crypto rules on promotions (Coin),
medical claims (Gene), export-control wording (Shield, Drone).
