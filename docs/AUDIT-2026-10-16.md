# Audit of all fourteen sites, 16 October 2026

Rule applied: `docs/SOURCING.md`. Each claim was searched or fetched on the day. "Verified" means a source supported it. "Changed" means the page
was wrong, out of date or unsupported and was corrected. "Not re-checked" means the claim is common knowledge or comes from the project's own
studies and was not fetched again this session. Where sources disagreed, the page now says so.

## Quantum

- **Title.** The headline "The machine that computes with atoms" did not say quantum computing, and superconducting and photonic machines do not
  compute with atoms. Now: "Quantum computing: what works, and what is a press release". The browser title is now "Frontier Quantum: quantum computing".
- **Changed: listing dates.** The page said IonQ, Rigetti and D-Wave all listed in 2021. IonQ listed in October 2021, Rigetti in March 2022, D-Wave on 8 August 2022.
- **Changed: Quantinuum.** It was described as private and mostly owned by Honeywell. It listed on Nasdaq (QNT) on 4 June 2026 at $60 a share, raising about $1.68 billion, and
  Honeywell kept about half the voting power. A Quantinuum card was added. It has no scenario range because it has only months of price history.
- **Changed: logical qubits.** "About 50 error-corrected qubits shown" is now 48, Quantinuum's own figure for Helios in its filing, labelled a company figure.
- **Removed (unsupported):** "80,000 logical qubits by 2030" from the evidence ladder; the claim that a speed-up must exceed about 100 times to repay cooling.
- **Changed: national figures.** Germany's programme is given as two sources that differ. India's rupee figure was dropped (no source found). UK is 2.5 billion pounds over 2024 to 2034.
- **Added:** the 2025 Nobel Prize in Physics, DARPA's Quantum Benchmarking Initiative Stage B, Quantinuum's roadmap.
- **Verified:** Willow's below-threshold result; Quantum Echoes (Oct 2025); Gidney 2025 (under a million noisy qubits, under a week); NIST FIPS 203, 204, 205; Majorana 1 contested;
  IBM Starling 2029 and Blue Jay 2033; IonQ's roadmap (1,600 logical qubits in 2028, 80,000 in 2030, a company claim shown as such in the chart).
- **Not re-checked:** early history (1981 to 1999), vendor and country company lists, the power comparison table, Oxford Ionics closing (the page now says IonQ agreed to buy it).

- **Changed: energy.** Cambridge gives 138 TWh (2025 survey) and about 175 TWh (running index). The chart bar for "all data centres" is relabelled "data centres, AI and crypto" because the IEA's 460 TWh includes crypto.

- **Not re-checked:** country tax rules on the map (general knowledge), Terra's loss figure, the 2010 pizza purchase, the project's own 652-token study.

## Atom

- **Changed: reactors.** "About 440 operating, 9% of electricity" is now 413 at the end of 2025 and 8.4% of electricity in 2025 (IAEA).
- **Changed: US approvals.** The page said only NuScale's design was approved. The NRC has since approved NuScale's 77 MWe design, TerraPower's Natrium construction permit (March 2026) and TVA's BWRX-300 at Clinch River (29 September 2026).
- **Changed: Ireland.** Section 18(6) of the Electricity Regulation Act 1999 is quoted. A bill to remove the prohibition was brought in May 2026. The government says it has no plans to authorise a plant. Data centres were 23% of metered electricity in 2025.
- **Changed: Switzerland.** The Federal Council proposed lifting the ban on new plants in August 2025, not 2024.
- **Not re-checked:** developers' licensing detail beyond the NRC items above, history dates, the cost range for new nuclear.

## Cell

- **Changed: pack price.** BloombergNEF's December 2025 survey: $108 per kWh (a record low), stationary storage $70. The 2024 baseline is quoted inconsistently, so the page no longer states one.
- **Changed: lithium.** Battery-grade lithium carbonate was about $19,750 a tonne in September 2026 (Benchmark), more than double the 2025 US average of about $9,000. The page had said it was down four-fifths.
- **Changed: China's cell share** is more than 80% in 2025 (IEA's 2026 outlook), not "about 75%".
- **Changed: companies.** QuantumScape amended its PowerCo agreement (maximum payments from about $131 million to $75.4 million) and added Honda; Eos Q2 2026 revenue $68.8 million with a $807 million backlog;
  Fluence's quarter to June 2026 had revenue of about $650 million, a 5.1% gross margin and a $6.4 billion backlog; Enovix quarterly revenue about $9.0 million.
- **Changed: Northvolt** filed for bankruptcy in March 2025 and Lyten agreed to buy its assets. "Grid batteries double in a year" was not supported and was removed.
- **Not re-checked:** chemistry table ranges, history dates before 2000.
