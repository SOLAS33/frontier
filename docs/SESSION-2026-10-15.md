## Operations learned
- `npx wrangler` fails on the founder PC (npm lock error). Install wrangler in a temp folder and run
  `node node_modules/wrangler/bin/wrangler.js deploy`. A shim for `npx wrangler` is described in OPERATIONS.md.
- Always `git fetch` and rebase solas33web before deploying it. A stale deploy removed Sheaf from the live products page for
  about ten minutes on 15 Oct.
- Classifier: headline attribution needs the company name, comparison and analyst headlines are ignored, and event types with
  no stated direction never move the odds.
